# Asset Credits and Provenance

All assets currently stored under `assets/source/` were downloaded on October 16, 2026 and are listed by their publishers as [Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Attribution is generally not required under CC0, but creator credits are retained here as thanks and to preserve provenance.

This record applies to the unmodified source library. Before adding new assets, verify the license on the individual source page and extend this file.

Music source files are stored in `assets/source/opengameart/music/`. The Town Theme source page requests the text `cynicmusic.com pixelsphere.org`; preserve that voluntary credit in the released game's credits.

The current prototype loops `town-theme.mp3` during Level 1. Combat uses `knifeSlice2.ogg` and `dropLeather.ogg` from Kenney's RPG Audio pack; the runtime copies under `public/assets/audio/` were converted to MP3 for broad mobile-browser playback.
